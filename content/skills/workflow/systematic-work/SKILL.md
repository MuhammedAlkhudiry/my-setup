## Workflow

1. Define exactly what the work covers.
2. List every item from the strongest available source, then search for omissions.
3. Classify every discovered item as `in scope`, `out of scope`, or `unclear`.
4. Split large sets into groups without overlap and keep one master list. When the work spans sessions, save it with $saved-work as type
   `tracker`.
5. Resolve every in-scope item, rerun discovery to catch omissions, then run relevant verification.
6. Record the outcome for every in-scope item. Report how coverage was checked, what was included, counts, verification results, and unresolved
   issues.

“All” means every discovered item. Passing checks do not prove that every item was reviewed.
